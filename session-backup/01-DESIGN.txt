Final design decision: eight complete independent oscillators. LEFT has L1 L2 L3 L4 and produces X. RIGHT has R1 R2 R3 R4 and produces Y. Every oscillator keeps waveform, base frequency, quarter-tone offset, phase, amplitude, offset, polarity, modulation, mute, solo, gain and enable. Quarter-tone steps are 50 cents. Locked mode shares a master base frequency; Free mode gives each generator its own base frequency. Do not reinterpret these as waveform segments.
